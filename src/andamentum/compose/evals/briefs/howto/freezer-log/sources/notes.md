# Freezer log notes

The Apollo Labs shared freezer must stay at or below minus 70 degrees Celsius.
The logger is a small probe that is placed on the middle shelf, not the door shelf.
Set the logger to record every 10 minutes and download the data every Friday.
If the temperature rises above minus 60 degrees Celsius, the alarm sounds and the duty officer must be called within 15 minutes.
Samples must be moved to the backup freezer in Room 12 if the freezer cannot reach minus 70 degrees within two hours.
Every download is saved in the lab shared folder under the freezer name and date.
