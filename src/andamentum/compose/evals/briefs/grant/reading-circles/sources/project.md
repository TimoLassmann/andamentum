# Reading-circles project notes

Lead: Dr Sam Reed, Harbor Library Trust (fictional).
The project will run weekly reading circles for adults in four neighbourhood libraries in the fictional city of Harbor Bay.
A 2023 library survey in Harbor Bay found that 27 percent of adult visitors said they rarely finish a book.
A pilot circle at one library ran for 12 weeks with 14 participants; 11 attended at least 9 sessions.
The project will train eight volunteer facilitators and run circles for 10 months.
Evaluation will use attendance records and a short survey at the start and end.
Budget details have not been settled.
