# Soil-sensor project notes

Principal investigator: Dr Jane Doe, Apollo University (fictional).
The project will build low-cost soil moisture sensors and test them on six small farms near the fictional town of Marlow.
A pilot on one farm in the previous season found that sensors cost 22 dollars each to build and ran for 140 days on one battery.
Farmers on the pilot farm reduced irrigation by about 15 percent according to their own records.
The team will build 120 sensors, place 20 on each farm, and collect data for nine months.
Data and sensor designs will be published openly after the project.
Budget details have not been settled.
