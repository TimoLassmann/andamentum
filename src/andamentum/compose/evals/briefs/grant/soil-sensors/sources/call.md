# Fictional Foundation small grants round

Grants are up to 40,000 dollars for projects of up to 12 months.
Applications must have these sections: Summary, Aims, Approach, Team, Budget, and Expected impact.
The Summary is limited to 150 words.
Projects must show how results will be shared openly with the public.
Applicants must state the evidence for the need and must not claim results they do not have.
