# Reading intervention trial notes

The trial enrolled 120 children aged 7 to 8 from six fictional primary schools and assigned them at random to two groups.
The intervention group received three 20-minute small-group reading sessions per week for ten weeks.
The control group continued normal classroom teaching.
Reading fluency was measured in words read correctly per minute at baseline and after ten weeks.
The intervention group improved by a mean of 14.6 words per minute and the control group by 6.1 words per minute.
Eleven children left the trial early, six from the control group and five from the intervention group.
Results come from one school year in one region and need replication.
