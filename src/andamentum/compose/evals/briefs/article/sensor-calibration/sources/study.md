# Sensor calibration study notes

The study tested the calibration of 30 low-cost temperature sensors against a reference thermometer.
Sensors were placed in a climate chamber and tested at 5, 15, 25, 35 and 45 degrees Celsius.
Each temperature was held for 30 minutes before readings were taken.
The mean absolute error across all sensors was 0.42 degrees Celsius.
Error grew at higher temperatures: the mean absolute error at 45 degrees Celsius was 0.71 degrees Celsius.
A linear correction fitted on 15 sensors cut the mean absolute error on the other 15 sensors to 0.19 degrees Celsius.
The study was limited to one sensor model and did not test humidity effects.
