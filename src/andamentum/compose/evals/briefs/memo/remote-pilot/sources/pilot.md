# Remote-work pilot results

The Apollo Labs remote-work pilot ran from 1 September to 30 November with 48 staff in three teams.
Median time to close a support ticket fell from 9.4 hours to 7.8 hours during the pilot.
Staff survey response rate was 91 percent; 72 percent said they were more productive at home.
Office rent costs for the three teams fell by 18 percent because two floors were sublet.
Two teams reported weaker onboarding for new hires: 5 of 9 new hires said they lacked a mentor.
The pilot cost 14,000 dollars in equipment, mostly monitors and headsets.
Options considered were to end the pilot, extend it for six months, or make it permanent.
