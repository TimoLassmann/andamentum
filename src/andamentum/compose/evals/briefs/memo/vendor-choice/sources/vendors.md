# Label printer vendor comparison

Vendor Birchline quoted 1,200 dollars per printer for 10 printers, with a three-year warranty and next-day support.
Vendor Cobalt Press quoted 950 dollars per printer for 10 printers, with a one-year warranty and five-day support.
Birchline printers print 200 labels per minute; Cobalt Press printers print 140 labels per minute.
The SaltLog packing line needs at least 150 labels per minute to keep up with peak orders.
Cobalt Press can deliver in two weeks; Birchline needs six weeks.
The current printers fail in the second week of July, when the lease ends.
