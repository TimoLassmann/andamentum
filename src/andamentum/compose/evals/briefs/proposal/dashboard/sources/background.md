# Distributor background

The distributor, Riverbend Supply, answers about 220 customer calls per week asking where a delivery is.
Each call takes about six minutes of staff time.
The SaltLog dashboard shows live delivery status to customers and staff and is built on the distributor's existing driver app.
The dashboard costs 18,000 dollars to set up and 900 dollars per month to run.
Setup takes ten weeks. SaltLog expects to cut status calls by about half.
Risks are that drivers must keep the app open and that customers may not adopt the portal.
