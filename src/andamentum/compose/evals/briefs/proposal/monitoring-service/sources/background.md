# Harbor Foods cold-chain background

Harbor Foods lost 31 pallets of frozen goods last year because of undetected temperature excursions in transit.
Each pallet is worth about 2,400 dollars.
Apollo Labs offers a sensor and alert service: one sensor per pallet, readings every five minutes, alerts to a phone within two minutes.
The service costs 3 dollars per pallet per trip and a 5,000 dollar one-time setup fee.
Setup takes four weeks, followed by a two-month trial on one route.
Risks noted by Apollo Labs are patchy mobile coverage on two rural routes and the need to train drivers.
