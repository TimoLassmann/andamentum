# Warehouse energy audit notes

The audit covered the SaltLog main warehouse from January to June.
Cold storage accounts for 58 percent of electricity use; lighting accounts for 21 percent.
Lighting is still fluorescent tubes in 14 of the 20 aisles.
Replacing the tubes with LED fixtures would cost 36,000 dollars and save an estimated 11,000 kilowatt hours per month.
Cold storage doors were found open for an average of 22 minutes per hour during loading.
Strip curtains on the cold storage doors would cost 4,500 dollars.
The facilities manager must decide which measures to fund this financial year.
