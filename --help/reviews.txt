Reviewer 1

1. The paper says daily logging lowered sodium intake. With no control group
this is an association over time, not an effect of logging. Please reword the
claim or add the limitation.

2. How was sodium estimated from the logged meals? Please name the
food-composition table and say how portion sizes were handled.

3. Table 1 is mentioned in the Methods but the paper never reports the
week-by-week means in the text. Please add them.

Reviewer 2

1. The completion rate should be reported, with the number of participants
who enrolled.

2. Please say whether the study was registered before recruitment started.
